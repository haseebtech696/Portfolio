About

My journey into AI Engineering

I'm a Computer Science student and Software Developer, building my way toward AI Engineering one practical project at a time — not by learning tools in isolation, but by learning how they work together.

Around two years ago, I started exploring YouTube automation and began learning how different tools and workflows could be used to automate content-related tasks. This introduced me to automation, APIs, data processing, and building systems that could handle repetitive work.

As I continued learning, I shifted my focus toward Web Scraping and Data Extraction, where I started working with Python and different techniques for collecting, processing, and organizing information from websites.

From there, I became increasingly interested in Workflow Automation and API Integration. I started working with automation platforms and tools such as n8n, connecting different applications, APIs, databases, AI models, and business workflows to create practical automated systems.

My learning journey is now moving toward AI Engineering. I am building my knowledge across AI tools, LLMs, machine learning, automation platforms, APIs, backend development, databases, and AI-powered applications.

What makes my journey different is that I am not focused on learning a single technology in isolation — I am learning how different technologies and tools can work together to solve real-world problems.

My goal is to become an AI Engineer and Software Developer capable of building complete intelligent systems — from data collection and backend development to automation, AI integration, and user-facing applications. Along the way I've worked with Python, web scraping, n8n, APIs, FastAPI, Streamlit, databases, AI/LLM tools, and machine learning technologies, and I'm continuously expanding this toolkit as I move deeper into AI Engineering.
